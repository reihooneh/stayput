Which of your furniture falls in an earthquake?

In strong shaking, tall furniture topples onto beds, desks and doorways. Stayput simulates hundreds of earthquakes on the floor you live on, works out what falls, where it lands, and which straps to put up first.

    How it works

    Draw your room

    Place your bookcases, wardrobes and shelves, the bed or desk, and the door. Say which floor you live on and what the ground is like.

    Shake it hundreds of times

    Each piece of furniture rocks on its edges under realistic earthquakes, filtered through your building's sway, from gentle to violent shaking.

    Strap what matters first

    You get the chance each item falls, whether it lands on someone or blocks the door, and an order to anchor things in.
