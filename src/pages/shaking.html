Taller and slimmer means it starts rocking sooner: it begins to tip when the floor's acceleration passes g times its depth over the height of its centre of mass. But bigger things of the same shape are harder to topple, because a big box rocks slowly and the shaking reverses before it gets past its tipping point. That is Housner's size effect, and it is why a tiny ornament falls before a wardrobe of the same proportions.
